Selection Criterion : Highest F1 on held-out test data
Engineering Justification:
In wildfire detection systems, False Negatives (predicting 'not fire' when a real wildfire
is raging) lead to catastrophic ecological and human loss. The F1-score provides a balanced
harmonic mean of Precision and Recall, avoiding accuracy illusions caused by slight class imbalance.
